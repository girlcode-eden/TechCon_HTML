Our History

TechCon began in 2010 as a grassroots gathering of passionate developers and tech enthusiasts in San Francisco. Over the years, it has evolved into a global event welcoming thousands of innovators, entrepreneurs, and thought leaders annually. With each edition, TechCon pushes the boundaries of technology discussion, showcasing cutting-edge trends and breakthroughs.

Our Mission

TechCon's mission is to inspire innovation, foster collaboration, and accelerate technological progress by bringing together the brightest minds across all tech disciplines. We are committed to creating an inclusive platform where diversity, creativity, and technical expertise flourish.

Notable Past Speakers

Dr. Ada Lovelace

Known as the first computer programmer, Dr. Lovelace spoke at TechCon 2015 about the foundational concepts of computational theory and how her early work influences modern software architecture.

Linus Torvalds

Creator of the Linux kernel, Linus shared insights in 2018 on open-source development and the future of software collaboration across international communities.

Ginni Rometty

Former CEO of IBM, Ginni delivered a keynote in 2020 about AI ethics and responsible innovation in enterprise technology.
